Commands: Comands Comand
        | Comand

Comand: IDEN EQ Expr
        | EXCLAMATION Expr
        | QUESTION IDEN
        | STAR 
        | HASH

Expr: Expr ADD Term
        | Expr SUB Term
        | Term

Term:| Expr MUL Factor
        | Expr DIV Factor
        | Factor

Factor: INT
        | ( Expr )
        | IDEN
